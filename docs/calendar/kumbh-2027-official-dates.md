# Kumbh 2027: official bathing dates (research record)

Read 2026-11-14 from the two state government websites. Not yet in the app, the backend calendar or the website journal.
Dates below are copied from the official pages; nothing is derived from the Panchang. Re-check both pages before publishing,
because neither shows a dated government notification and both can change.

## Haridwar (Uttarakhand)

Source: https://haridwarkumbh.uk.gov.in ("Important Snan Dates", all at Har Ki Pauri). The site calls the event
"Haridwar Kumbh 2027"; the Mela administration and news wires call it "Ardh Kumbh Mela" (Haridwar's last full Kumbh was 2021).

| 8 Mar 2027 | Phalguna Amavasya | Amrit Snan |
| 15 Mar 2027 | Dev Doli | Amrit Snan |
| 7 Apr 2027 | Nav Samvatsar | |

| 15 Apr 2027 | Shri Ram Navami | |
| 20 Apr 2027 | Chaitra Purnima | |

Discrepancy (unresolved): the Mela Officer's announcement reported by PTI on 2026-04-14 listed 10 baths with 3 Amrit Snans
(6 Mar, 8 Mar, 14 Apr). The official site lists 11, adding 15 Mar "Dev Doli" as a fourth Amrit Snan. The CM's announcement of
2025-11-28 (Garhwal Post) matches the 10-bath list. Treat 15 Mar as unverified until a dated notice confirms it.
Not stated on the site: the formal start and end of the Mela period.

## Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Simhastha (Maharashtra)

Source: https://simhasthamahakumbh.maharashtra.gov.in/snan-calendar and /amrut-snan-calendar-2027.

| Date | Event | Place |
|---|---|---|
| 31 Oct 2026, 12:02 PM | Dhwajarohan (flag hoisting), start of Simhastha | |
| 15 Jun 2027 | Major Mela period begins | |
| 29 Jul 2027 | Nagar Pradakshina (procession, not a snan; time "to be notified") | Nashik |
| 2 Aug 2027 | First Amrut Snan (Ashadh Somvati Amavasya) | Nashik |
| 31 Aug 2027 | Second Amrut Snan, "Mahakumbh Snan" (Shravan Amavasya) | Nashik |
| 11 Sep 2027 | Third Amrut Snan (Vaishnava akhadas) | Ramtirth, Nashik |
| 12 Sep 2027 | Third Amrut Snan (Shaiva akhadas) | Kushavarta Tirth, Trimbakeshwar |
| 24 Jul 2028, 3:36 PM | Dhwajavatarana (flag lowering), end | |

The site also lists 65 parvakal muhurat days at Ramtirth and 41 at Kushavarta. It says its tithi and planetary explanations
are almanac calculations and that the Akhada Parishad and government announcements are final. Some news reports call
29 Jul 2027 the first Amrit Snan; the official site says it is the Nagar Pradakshina.

## Governance notes before anything ships (AGENTS.md sections 2 and 3)

  profile-qualified Panchang occurrences, so they must not be recomputed per user location.
